# Banana & Plantain Cultivation in Sri Lanka

## 1. Popular Cultivars
- **Embul (AAB group)**: Acid banana, highly popular local dessert variety.
- **Kolikuttu (AAB group)**: Premium dessert banana, high market price.
- **Seeni Kesel (ABB group)**: Drought resistant, medicinal value.
- **Ambon (AAA group)**: Cavendish type, large bunches for export and supermarket retail.
- **Analu / Rath Kesel**: Red banana variety rich in antioxidants.

## 2. Soil & Planting Material
- Deep, well-drained loamy soil rich in organic matter.
- Tissue cultured plants or healthy sword suckers weighing 1.5-2.0 kg.
- Pit size: 60 cm x 60 cm x 60 cm filled with topsoil, compost (10 kg), and 200g Rock Phosphate.

## 3. Spacing & Density
- Embul & Kolikuttu: 2.4 m x 2.4 m (approx. 700 plants per acre).
- Cavendish/Ambon: 3.0 m x 2.4 m (approx. 550 plants per acre).

## 4. Pest & Disease Management
- **Banana Bunchy Top Virus (BBTV)**: Transmitted by banana aphid (Pentalonia nigronervosa). Uproot and burn infected mats immediately.
- **Panama Disease (Fusarium Wilt)**: Soil-borne fungus affecting Kolikuttu. Use resistant cultivars or solarize soil.
- **Banana Weevil Borer**: Use clean suckers paired with traps (cut stem sections).

## 5. Cost & Profitability per Acre (1st Year Estimate)
- Initial land prep & pits: LKR 60,000
- Planting material (700 suckers): LKR 105,000
- Fertilizer & manure: LKR 90,000
- Labor & Irrigation: LKR 120,000
- Total 1st Year Cost: ~LKR 375,000
- Expected 1st Year Yield: 600 bunches (~12,000 kg) @ LKR 120/kg = ~LKR 1,440,000 Gross Revenue.
