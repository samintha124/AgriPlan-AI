# Agricultural Cost Estimation & Government Subsidies in Sri Lanka

## 1. Government Subsidy Programs (Department of Agriculture)
- **Paddy Fertilizer Subsidy**: Financial cash grant of LKR 15,000 to LKR 25,000 per hectare per season directly transferred to farmers' bank accounts.
- **Micro-Irrigation Subsidy**: 50% to 75% subsidy provided for drip irrigation systems and solar water pumping systems under the Agri-Entrepreneurship Development Scheme.

## 2. Average Input Costs (2025/2026 Reference Data)
- **Commercial Organic Compost**: LKR 35 - 50 per kg.
- **Urea (46% N)**: LKR 9,000 - 11,000 per 50kg bag.
- **Muriate of Potash (MOP)**: LKR 14,000 - 16,000 per 50kg bag.
- **Triple Super Phosphate (TSP)**: LKR 12,000 - 15,000 per 50kg bag.
- **Agricultural Labor Rate**: LKR 2,500 - 3,500 per man-day depending on district and task (land prep, harvesting, spraying).
- **Tractor Machinery Hire**: LKR 8,000 - 12,000 per acre for primary land preparation (4-wheel 2-tine / disk plow).

## 3. Water Management Guidelines
- Drip Irrigation: Saves 40-60% water compared to flood irrigation. Recommended for high-value cash crops (Chilli, Tomato, Melon, Papaya).
- Sprinkler Irrigation: Ideal for leafy vegetables, onion, and potato fields in Upcountry (Nuwara Eliya, Badulla).
