# Sri Lankan Rice (Paddy) Cultivation Guide - Department of Agriculture

## 1. Agro-Ecological Zones & Seasons
In Sri Lanka, paddy cultivation mainly occurs during two major monsoon seasons:
- **Maha Season**: Starts with the North-East monsoon from September/October to March.
- **Yala Season**: Starts with the South-West monsoon from April to August/September.

Major paddy growing regions include Polonnaruwa, Anuradhapura, Ampara, Kurunegala, and Hambantota.

## 2. Seed Selection & Varieties
- **Bg 352 & Bg 358**: 3.5-month varieties recommended for dry and intermediate zones. High yield potential.
- **Bg 300 & Bg 305**: 3-month short-duration varieties suitable for water-deficit areas.
- **At 362**: Red pericarp variety resistant to bacterial leaf blight, popular in Southern Sri Lanka.
- **Traditional Varieties**: Suwandel, Kalu Heenati, and Pachchaperumal - cultivated organically with high market value.

## 3. Land Preparation & Seed Rate
- Initial plowing (primary tillage) followed by 7-10 days of flooding to break down organic weed mass.
- Secondary plowing and leveling for uniform water distribution.
- Recommended seeding rate for direct seeding: 100-125 kg per hectare (40-50 kg per acre).

## 4. Fertilizer Management (DOA Recommendations)
- **Basal Fertilizer**: Apply Muriate of Potash (MOP) and Triple Super Phosphate (TSP) or Rock Phosphate before final leveling.
- **Top Dressing Urea**:
  - 1st Top Dressing: 2 weeks after broadcasting (Tiller initiation stage).
  - 2nd Top Dressing: 4-5 weeks after broadcasting (Panicle initiation stage).
  - 3rd Top Dressing: 7 weeks after broadcasting (Heading stage).
- Recommended dosage per acre: Urea 65-80 kg, TSP 25 kg, MOP 30 kg depending on soil test results.

## 5. Water & Weed Control
- Maintain 2-5 cm water layer after crop establishment until 2 weeks before harvest.
- Weed management: Apply pre-emergence herbicide (Pyrazosulfuron-ethyl) within 3-5 days after broadcasting.
